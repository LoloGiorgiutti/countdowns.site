Glastonbury Festival is the world's most iconic outdoor music and arts festival, held at Worthy Farm in Pilton, Somerset, UK. The 2027 festival runs from Wednesday, June 23 to Sunday, June 27. Note: there is no Glastonbury in 2026 — the festival observes a fallow year every few years to allow the farmland to recover.

Preguntas Frecuentes

When is Glastonbury 2027?

Glastonbury Festival 2027 runs Wednesday, June 23 to Sunday, June 27, 2027 at Worthy Farm in Pilton, Somerset, UK.

Is there a Glastonbury in 2026?

No — there is no Glastonbury Festival in 2026. The festival observes a fallow year to allow the farmland and cows to recover. The next Glastonbury is in 2027.

How do I get Glastonbury tickets?

Glastonbury tickets are sold through an annual registration and ballot system. Registration is free but demand far exceeds supply.

Who has headlined Glastonbury?

Past headliners include Beyoncé, Paul McCartney, Coldplay, Radiohead, Adele, David Bowie, The Rolling Stones, and Stormzy.
