The BAFTA Film Awards are presented annually by the British Academy of Film and Television Arts to honor the best in cinema. First held in 1948, the BAFTAs are one of the world's most prestigious film awards and often serve as a strong predictor of Oscar outcomes. The 80th BAFTA Film Awards ceremony takes place on Sunday, February 21, 2027 at the Royal Festival Hall, Southbank Centre, London.
