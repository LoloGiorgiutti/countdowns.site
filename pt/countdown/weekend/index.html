Perguntas Frequentes

How many days until the weekend?

Use the live countdown above to see the exact days, hours, minutes and seconds until Saturday. It updates automatically based on your local time.

What day does the weekend start?

This countdown uses Saturday at midnight (00:00) as the start of the weekend.

Does the countdown use my local time?

Yes — the countdown uses your device's local timezone, so it's accurate wherever you are in the world.

How many hours until Friday?

If you want a countdown specifically to Friday, use the custom countdown feature on this site to set your own target date and time.
