New York Fashion Week (NYFW) is held twice a year in New York City — in February (for Fall/Winter collections) and September (for Spring/Summer collections). It is the first of the four major fashion weeks, followed by London, Milan and Paris.

Perguntas Frequentes

When is New York Fashion Week 2026?

NYFW September 2026 starts on September 8, 2026 (Spring/Summer 2027 collections).

Can I attend NYFW?

NYFW shows are primarily industry-only events (buyers, press, stylists). Some designers hold open presentations or pop-up events — check nyfw.com for public programming.

How long does New York Fashion Week last?

NYFW typically runs for about 7–9 days. It is held twice a year: February (Fall/Winter) and September (Spring/Summer).

What is the order of the Big Four fashion weeks?

The Big Four run in order: New York → London → Milan → Paris. The September season typically starts in early September and ends in late October.
