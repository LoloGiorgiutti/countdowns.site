Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

countdowns.site ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website https://countdowns.site. This page explains how we handle information when you use our site.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information. The site works entirely in your browser. We do not require registration or login.

We store the following data locally in your browser (localStorage) to remember your preferences:

  • Your selected language (cd_lang_override)
  • Your selected country (cd_country)
  • Your display theme (light/dark)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Analytics

We may use anonymous analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how users interact with our site. These tools collect aggregated, anonymous data such as pages visited, session duration, and geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected.

4. External Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us using the feedback form available on any countdown page on this site.
