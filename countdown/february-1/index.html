Frequently Asked Questions

How many days until February 1st?

Use the live countdown above to see the exact days, hours, minutes and seconds until February 1st.

What day of the year is February 1st?

February 1st is day number 32 of the year (in a non-leap year). It falls in week 5 of the ISO calendar year.

What week of the year is February 1st?

February 1st falls in ISO week 5 of the year. It is day 32 of the calendar year.

Does the countdown reset each year?

Yes — once February 1 arrives, the countdown automatically resets to next year's February 1.

Is February 1 a holiday?

It depends on the country. Use the live countdown above to count down to February 1, regardless of whether it is a public holiday.
