Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) is a Mexican and Latin American holiday celebrated on November 1–2, coinciding with the Catholic holy days of All Saints' Day and All Souls' Day. It honours deceased loved ones with colourful altars, marigolds, skull imagery and celebrations.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Día de los Muertos 2026?

Día de los Muertos is celebrated November 1–2, 2026.

Is Día de los Muertos the same as Halloween?

No. While they fall close together, Día de los Muertos is a distinct Mexican tradition honouring deceased loved ones, not a horror celebration.
