WWE Survivor Series is one of the original Big Four WWE premium live events, first held in 1987. Known for its traditional elimination tag team matches — Survivor Series matches — it typically takes place in November. The event has evolved over the years and now often features brand-vs-brand competition between Raw and SmackDown. The 2026 date has not yet been announced.

What is a Survivor Series match?

A Survivor Series match is a traditional elimination tag team match where teams of 4 or 5 superstars compete, with the winning team being the last to have remaining members.

How long has Survivor Series been held?

WWE Survivor Series has been held annually since 1987, making it one of the longest-running events in WWE history.
