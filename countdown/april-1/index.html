April 1st is day 91 of the year (out of 365) and falls in week 14 of the ISO calendar. After this date, 274 days remain until the end of the year. People born on April 1 are born under the sign of Aries. In the Northern Hemisphere, April 1 falls in Spring; in the Southern Hemisphere it falls in Autumn. April Fools' Day: April Fools' Day on April 1 is celebrated with pranks, hoaxes, and jokes in many countries around the world.
