The World Series is the annual championship series of Major League Baseball (MLB), contested between the American League and National League champions. First held in 1903, it is one of the oldest professional sports championships in North America.

The series is played in a best-of-seven format, with the first team to win four games crowned World Series champions. The 2026 World Series is expected to take place in late October.
