Virat Kohli was born on November 5, in Delhi, India. Widely regarded as one of the greatest cricketers of all time, Kohli has broken numerous batting records across all three formats of the game. He made his international debut for India in 2008 and rose to become one of the most consistent run-scorers in cricket history, amassing over 27,000 international runs. He served as captain of the Indian national team across all formats and led India to numerous victories. Known for his aggressive playing style, outstanding fitness regime, and passionate on-field demeanour, he is the most-followed cricketer on social media with over 270 million Instagram followers. His performances in the Indian Premier League (IPL) for Royal Challengers Bangalore have been equally legendary.
