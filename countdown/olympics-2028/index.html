The 2028 Summer Olympic Games will be held in Los Angeles, California from July 14 to July 30, 2028. Los Angeles previously hosted the Olympics in 1932 and 1984. The Opening Ceremony takes place at the historic LA Memorial Coliseum.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the 2028 Olympics?

The LA 2028 Olympics run from July 14 to July 30, 2028. The Opening Ceremony is on July 14, 2028.

Where are the 2028 Olympics?

The 2028 Summer Olympics are hosted by Los Angeles, California, USA.

What new sports are in the 2028 Olympics?

LA 2028 will include cricket, flag football, baseball/softball, lacrosse and squash as new sports.

How do I get 2028 Olympics tickets?

Ticket sales are managed by LA28. Registration for priority access opens at la28.org.
