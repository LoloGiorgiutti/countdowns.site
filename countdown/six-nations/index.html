The Six Nations Championship is the annual international rugby union competition between England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland, and Wales. Matches are played on alternating home and away grounds, with each country hosting the others across multiple weekends.

The 2027 tournament begins on February 6, 2027. Ireland are the defending champions following dominant performances in recent years. The Triple Crown (awarded to the best home nation) and the Grand Slam (winning all matches) are additional prizes within the tournament.
