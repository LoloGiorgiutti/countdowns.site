William Bradley Pitt was born on December 18, in Shawnee, Oklahoma, USA. One of Hollywood's most iconic actors and producers, Pitt rose to fame with Thelma & Louise (1991) and became a bona fide superstar with roles in Se7en (1995), Fight Club (1999), Ocean's Eleven (2001), and Troy (2004). He earned Oscar nominations for The Curious Case of Benjamin Button and Moneyball before winning Best Supporting Actor for Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019). His production company Plan B Entertainment produced award-winning films including 12 Years a Slave. Outside acting, Pitt is known for his charitable work and his Miraval rosé wine brand.
