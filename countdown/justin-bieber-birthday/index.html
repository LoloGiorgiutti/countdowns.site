Justin Drew Bieber was born on March 1, 1994, in London, Ontario, Canada. Discovered by talent manager Scooter Braun at age 13 after posting YouTube videos, Bieber became one of the youngest solo artists ever to reach number one on the Billboard Hot 100. His debut studio album My World 2.0 (2010) launched him to global stardom. Over the following decade he released hit records including Believe, Purpose, and Changes, accumulating billions of streams and multiple Grammy Awards. Known for chart-topping singles such as Baby, Sorry, Love Yourself, and Peaches, Bieber has remained one of the most followed artists in the world. He married model Hailey Baldwin in 2018. His 33th birthday on March 1, 2027 will be celebrated by fans across every continent.
