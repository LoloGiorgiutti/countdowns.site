Midnight marks the transition between one calendar day and the next, occurring at exactly 12:00:00 AM. The countdown above resets automatically every day, always showing the time remaining until the next midnight in your selected country's timezone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is midnight?

Midnight is 12:00:00 AM, the moment when one day ends and the next begins.

Is midnight 12 AM or 12 PM?

Midnight is 12:00 AM (ante meridiem — before noon). Noon is 12:00 PM.

Does the countdown adjust for my timezone?

Yes. The countdown uses the timezone of your selected country to show the correct local midnight.
