Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior, known as Neymar Jr, was born on February 5, 1992 in Mogi das Cruzes, São Paulo, Brazil. He is widely considered one of the greatest footballers of his generation and is Brazil's all-time top scorer for the national team.

Neymar has played for Santos, FC Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, and Al-Hilal. He won the FIFA Ballon d'Or Bronze in 2015 and 2017. Despite injury setbacks, he remains one of the most followed athletes in the world with over 200 million Instagram followers.
