The US Open is the fourth and final Grand Slam of the tennis calendar, held each year at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing Meadows, New York. The 2026 edition runs from August 31 to September 13. Played on hard courts under New York's city lights, the US Open is known for its electric atmosphere and night sessions that attract top celebrities.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the US Open Tennis 2026?

The US Open 2026 begins Monday, August 31 and runs through Sunday, September 13, 2026 in New York.

Where is the US Open played?

The US Open is held at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing Meadows, Queens, New York City.

Who has won the most US Open titles?

Jimmy Connors, Pete Sampras, and Roger Federer each won the US Open five times in the men's draw; Chris Evert and Serena Williams each won six titles in the women's draw.
