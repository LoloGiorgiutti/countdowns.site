The FA Cup is the oldest football cup competition in the world, first held in 1871. The final is traditionally played at Wembley Stadium in London, usually in May, drawing over 86,000 fans and millions of TV viewers worldwide.

Any English club from the Premier League to the lower amateur divisions can enter, making famous cup upsets a hallmark of the competition.
