The Brazilian General Election of 2026 will be held on October 4, 2026, with a potential runoff on October 25, 2026. Brazilian voters will elect the President, Vice President, all 513 Federal Deputies, two-thirds of the 81 Federal Senators, and the Governors and state legislatures of all 26 states and the Federal District. The incumbent President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva may seek re-election. Brazil uses a two-round system for presidential elections.
