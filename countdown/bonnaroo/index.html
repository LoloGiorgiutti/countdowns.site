Bonnaroo Music and Arts Festival is held annually at Great Stage Park in Manchester, Tennessee. Founded in 2002, it is one of the largest and most acclaimed music festivals in the United States, spanning rock, hip-hop, electronic, folk, and everything in between. The 2026 festival runs June 11–14 across multiple stages and camping grounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Bonnaroo 2026?

Bonnaroo 2026 takes place June 11–14, 2026 at Great Stage Park in Manchester, Tennessee.

Can you camp at Bonnaroo?

Yes — camping is a central part of the Bonnaroo experience. General camping is included with festival tickets, and premium camping options are available for purchase.

What genres are featured at Bonnaroo?

Bonnaroo is one of the most eclectic festivals in the US, featuring rock, indie, hip-hop, electronic, R&B, country, folk, and comedy.
