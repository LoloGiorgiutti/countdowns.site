About Shakira's Birthday 2027

Shakira Isabel Mebarak Ripoll, known simply as Shakira, was born on February 2, 1977 in Barranquilla, Colombia. She is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with over 80 million records sold worldwide. Known for hits like 'Waka Waka', 'Hips Don't Lie', 'She Wolf', and her recent hit 'Bzrp Music Sessions #53', Shakira is a global icon.

In 2027, Shakira celebrates her milestone 50th birthday — a half century of the woman who put Colombian music on the world map. Her 2024 comeback after personal difficulties was one of the biggest stories in pop culture.
