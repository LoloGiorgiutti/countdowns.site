El Dia de la Revolucion is a Mexican national holiday celebrated on the third Monday of November (closest to November 20th) to commemorate the start of the Mexican Revolution in 1910. The revolution began as a movement against the dictatorship of Porfirio Diaz and transformed into a decade-long civil conflict that reshaped Mexico.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Dia de la Revolucion in Mexico?

Revolution Day in Mexico is observed on the third Monday of November (around November 20th).

What does Dia de la Revolucion celebrate?

It commemorates the start of the Mexican Revolution on November 20, 1910, led by Francisco I. Madero against Porfirio Diaz.

How is it celebrated?

Celebrations include military and civic parades, school ceremonies, and cultural events throughout Mexico.
