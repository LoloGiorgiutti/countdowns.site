Primavera Sound Sao Paulo is held at the Autodromo de Interlagos, the iconic Formula 1 venue. The 2026 edition is scheduled for December 5-6, featuring the same eclectic international lineup as the Barcelona and Buenos Aires editions.

When is Primavera Sound Sao Paulo 2026?

December 5-6, 2026 at Autodromo de Interlagos, Sao Paulo.

Where is Primavera Sound Sao Paulo held?

At the Autodromo Jose Carlos Pace (Interlagos), also home to the Brazilian Formula 1 Grand Prix.

How do I get tickets?

Tickets are available at primaverasound.com.br.
