February is the second month of the year and the shortest, with 28 days in common years and 29 in leap years. It hosts Valentine's Day on February 14th and Groundhog Day on February 2nd.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does February have?

February has 28 days in common years and 29 in leap years.

When is the next leap year?

Leap years occur every 4 years. The next leap year is 2028.

What holidays are in February?

Valentine's Day (Feb 14), Groundhog Day (Feb 2), Presidents' Day in the USA (third Monday), and Carnival in many countries.
