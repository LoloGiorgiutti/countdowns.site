The Melbourne Cup is Australia's most celebrated horse race, held on the first Tuesday of November at Flemington Racecourse in Melbourne. In 2026 it takes place on November 3. Known as 'The Race That Stops a Nation,' the Melbourne Cup is a national institution — a public holiday in metropolitan Melbourne, with sweepstake tickets sold across Australia and an estimated 5 million Australians tuning in to watch.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Melbourne Cup 2026?

The Melbourne Cup 2026 is held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. It is always run on the first Tuesday of November at Flemington Racecourse, Melbourne.

Is Melbourne Cup Day a public holiday?

Yes — Melbourne Cup Day is a public holiday in metropolitan Melbourne and some regional areas of Victoria. Many other states observe an unofficial half-day or stop for the race.

How long is the Melbourne Cup race?

The Melbourne Cup is run over a distance of 3,200 metres (2 miles), making it one of the longest major flat races in the world.
