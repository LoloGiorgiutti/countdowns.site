Selena Marie Gomez was born on July 22, 1992, in Grand Prairie, Texas, USA. She began her career as a child actress on Barney & Friends before rising to fame on the Disney Channel series Wizards of Waverly Place. Transitioning to music, she released successful albums with her band The Scene and later as a solo artist, charting hits including Come & Get It, Good for You, Bad Liar, and Lose You to Love Me. Her personal life — including a high-profile kidney transplant in 2017 due to lupus — has made her a powerful advocate for mental health awareness. She also stars in and produces the hit comedy-mystery series Only Murders in the Building.
