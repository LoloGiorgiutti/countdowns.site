Labor Day in the United States and Canada is celebrated on the first Monday of September. It honors the labour movement and the contributions of workers. In the US, it also marks the unofficial end of summer.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Labor Day?

Labor Day 2026 falls on Monday, September 7.

Is Labor Day the same as International Workers' Day?

No. International Workers' Day (May Day) is May 1. US/Canada Labor Day is the first Monday of September.

Is Labor Day a federal holiday?

Yes, Labor Day is a federal holiday in both the United States and Canada. Most businesses, schools and government offices are closed.

Does Labor Day mark the end of summer?

Yes. Labor Day weekend is considered the unofficial end of summer in the US, and many schools resume in the days following it.
