Black Friday is the Friday after Thanksgiving in the United States and has become the unofficial start of the Christmas shopping season. Retailers offer steep discounts, and the day has expanded globally with online deals that now extend over an entire week.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, 2026.

When does Black Friday start?

Many retailers now start Black Friday deals a week early online. The main in-store event is the Friday after US Thanksgiving.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Cyber Monday 2026 is on Monday, November 30, 2026 — three days after Black Friday.
