September is the ninth month of the year with 30 days. The autumn equinox in the Northern Hemisphere falls around September 22 or 23. Schools typically resume in September across the Northern Hemisphere.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does autumn start in September?

The autumn equinox in the Northern Hemisphere falls around September 22 or 23.

What is celebrated in September?

Labor Day in the USA and Canada (first Monday), Mexican Independence Day (Sep 16), and the autumn equinox.

How many days does September have?

September has 30 days.
