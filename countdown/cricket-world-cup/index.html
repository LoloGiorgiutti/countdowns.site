About ICC Cricket World Cup 2027

The ICC Cricket World Cup 2027 will be hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe, and Namibia. The tournament features the world's top cricketing nations competing in the ODI (One Day International) format — each team plays 50 overs per innings.

Cricket is the world's second most popular sport with over 2.5 billion fans, particularly in India, Pakistan, Australia, England, the Caribbean, and South Africa.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Cricket World Cup 2027?

Exact dates are not yet confirmed. The tournament is scheduled for 2027 in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia.

What format is the Cricket World Cup?

It's played in ODI format — One Day Internationals with 50 overs per side.

Who are the top contenders?

India, Australia, England, Pakistan, and New Zealand are historically the strongest teams.
