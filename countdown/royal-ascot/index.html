Royal Ascot is a five-day horse racing festival held each June at Ascot Racecourse in Berkshire, attended by the British Royal Family. The 2026 meeting runs June 16–20. Renowned for its strict dress code, elaborate hats, and world-class flat racing, Royal Ascot attracts over 280,000 racegoers and more than 300 horses from across the globe. The Gold Cup on Ladies' Day (Thursday) is the social highlight of the week.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Royal Ascot 2026?

Royal Ascot 2026 runs from Tuesday June 16 to Saturday June 20, 2026 at Ascot Racecourse in Berkshire, England.

What is the dress code at Royal Ascot?

Royal Ascot has strict dress codes by enclosure. The Royal Enclosure requires formal morning dress for men and dresses or skirts with a hat or headpiece for women. Other enclosures have formal to smart casual requirements.

What are the biggest races at Royal Ascot?

The Gold Cup (Group 1, 2.5 miles) on Thursday is the most prestigious race. Other highlights include the Queen Anne Stakes, St James's Palace Stakes, and the Royal Hunt Cup.
