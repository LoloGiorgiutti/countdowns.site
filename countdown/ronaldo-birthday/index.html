Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. Known worldwide as CR7, he is one of the greatest footballers in history, having won five Ballon d'Or awards and five UEFA Champions League titles with Manchester United, Real Madrid, and during his career at Juventus. Ronaldo holds the record for most international goals scored in men's football, representing Portugal. After stints in England, Spain, and Italy, he joined Al-Nassr FC in Saudi Arabia in 2023.
