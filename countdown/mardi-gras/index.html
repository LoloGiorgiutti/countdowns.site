Mardi Gras — French for 'Fat Tuesday' — is the final day of the Carnival season, celebrated the day before Ash Wednesday. In 2027 it falls on February 9. New Orleans hosts the world's most famous Mardi Gras celebrations, with parades, beads, jazz music, and the iconic floats on Bourbon Street. The date changes each year as it is always 47 days before Easter Sunday.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Mardi Gras 2027?

Mardi Gras 2027 falls on Tuesday, February 9, 2027. It is always the day before Ash Wednesday and 47 days before Easter Sunday.

Why is it called Fat Tuesday?

The name 'Fat Tuesday' refers to the tradition of eating rich, fatty foods before the fasting and abstinence of the Lenten season that begins on Ash Wednesday.

Where are the biggest Mardi Gras celebrations?

The most famous Mardi Gras celebration is in New Orleans, Louisiana. Other major celebrations include Venice (Italy), Rio de Janeiro (Brazil), and cities in France and Belgium.
