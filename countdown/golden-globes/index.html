The Golden Globe Awards are presented annually by the Hollywood Foreign Press Association (HFPA), honoring the best in film and television from around the world. Known for their glamour and celebrity-studded red carpets, the Golden Globes are widely considered the unofficial start of Hollywood's awards season and often predict Oscar winners. When are the Golden Globes 2027?

The Golden Globes 2027 date has not yet been officially announced. The ceremony typically takes place in January in Beverly Hills, Los Angeles. Check back for updates.

What categories do the Golden Globes cover?

The Golden Globes cover both film and television, with separate categories for drama and comedy/musical in each medium. Major categories include Best Picture (Drama and Comedy), Best Director, Best Actor, and Best Actress.

How do the Golden Globes differ from the Oscars?

The Golden Globes cover both film and TV, are voted on by the Hollywood Foreign Press Association, and are known for a more festive atmosphere. The Oscars cover only film and are voted on by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences.
