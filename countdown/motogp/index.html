The MotoGP Italian Grand Prix at Mugello is one of the most iconic rounds of the MotoGP World Championship. The Mugello Circuit in Tuscany, Italy, is famous for its passionate fans (the tifosi) and high-speed layout. The 2026 race takes place on May 31 and is Round 8 of the 2026 MotoGP season — the final season of the 1000cc era.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the 2026 MotoGP Italian Grand Prix?

The 2026 MotoGP Italian GP (Mugello) race day is Sunday, May 31, 2026.

Where is the MotoGP Italian GP held?

The Italian Grand Prix takes place at the Mugello Circuit (Autodromo Internazionale del Mugello) in Tuscany, Italy.

What channel is MotoGP on?

MotoGP coverage varies by country. Check your local sports broadcaster for MotoGP live coverage.

How many rounds in the 2026 MotoGP season?

The 2026 MotoGP season consists of 22 rounds across 5 continents and 18 countries.
