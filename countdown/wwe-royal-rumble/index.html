The WWE Royal Rumble is one of WWE's most anticipated premium live events, famous for its eponymous Royal Rumble match — a 30-man (or 30-woman) over-the-top-rope battle royal where the winner earns a championship match at WrestleMania. Held annually in January, the Royal Rumble marks the official beginning of the Road to WrestleMania. The 2027 date has not yet been announced.

What is the Royal Rumble match?

The Royal Rumble match features 30 superstars who enter at timed intervals. The winner earns a title shot at WrestleMania.

How do you win the Royal Rumble?

You win the Royal Rumble by being the last superstar remaining after throwing all opponents over the top rope so both feet touch the floor.
