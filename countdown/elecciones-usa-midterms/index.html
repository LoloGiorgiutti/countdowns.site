The 2026 United States midterm elections will be held on November 3, 2026. Every two years, Americans vote for all 435 seats in the House of Representatives and one-third of the 100 Senate seats. These elections are a crucial midpoint check on the sitting president's party.

The results will determine which party controls Congress for the final two years of the presidential term, directly influencing legislation, budgets, and government oversight.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the 2026 US Midterm Elections?

The 2026 US Midterm Elections are on November 3, 2026.

What is voted on in the US Midterms?

All 435 House of Representatives seats and 34 of 100 Senate seats are up for election, plus many state and local races.

Why are midterms important?

Midterm elections determine which party controls Congress, which directly affects legislation and the president's agenda for the last two years of the term.
