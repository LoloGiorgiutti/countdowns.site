The League of Legends World Championship (Worlds) is the annual global tournament for the popular MOBA game by Riot Games. First held in 2011, Worlds brings together the top teams from each regional league — LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS, and more — to compete for the Summoner's Cup. The event typically takes place in October and November, attracting tens of millions of concurrent viewers. The 2026 host region and dates have not yet been announced.

What is the Summoner's Cup?

The Summoner's Cup is the trophy awarded to the League of Legends World Champions.

Which region has won the most World Championships?

South Korea's LCK region has produced the most World Champions, with T1 (formerly SKT T1) winning four titles.
