The BRIT Awards are the UK's most prestigious music awards, presented annually by the BPI (British Phonographic Industry). First held in 1977, the BRITs celebrate the best in British and international music across categories including Artist of the Year, Album of the Year, and Song of the Year. The ceremony typically takes place in February or March at The O2 Arena in London. The 2027 date has not yet been announced.
