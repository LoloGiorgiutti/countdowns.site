Canada Day (Fête du Canada) is Canada's national holiday, celebrated every year on July 1st. It marks the anniversary of Canadian Confederation on July 1, 1867, when the British North America Act united three colonies into a single dominion. In 2026, Canada celebrates its 159th birthday.

Canadians celebrate with fireworks, parades, concerts, and outdoor festivals. The largest celebrations take place in Ottawa (the capital) on Parliament Hill, as well as in major cities like Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and Calgary.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Canada Day 2026?

Canada Day is always on July 1. In 2026, it falls on Wednesday, July 1, 2026.

What does Canada Day celebrate?

Canada Day marks the anniversary of Canadian Confederation on July 1, 1867, when Canada became a self-governing dominion.

How is Canada Day celebrated?

With fireworks, parades, concerts, outdoor festivals, and community events across the country. Ottawa hosts the main national celebration on Parliament Hill.

Is Canada Day a public holiday?

Yes, Canada Day is a federal statutory holiday — all government offices, banks, and most businesses are closed.
