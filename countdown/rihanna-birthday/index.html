Robyn Rihanna Fenty was born on February 20, in Saint Michael, Barbados. One of the best-selling music artists of all time, Rihanna has sold over 250 million records worldwide. Discovered by record producer Evan Rogers at age 16, she signed with Def Jam Recordings and released her debut single Pon de Replay in 2005. Over the following years she released hit albums including Good Girl Gone Bad, Rated R, Loud, and Anti, producing iconic songs such as Umbrella, We Found Love, Diamonds, and Work. Beyond music, Rihanna is a highly successful entrepreneur — her Fenty Beauty cosmetics brand and Savage X Fenty lingerie line have become billion-dollar enterprises.
