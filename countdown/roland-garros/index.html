Roland Garros — known as the French Open — is the world's premier clay-court Grand Slam, held annually at Stade Roland Garros in the 16th arrondissement of Paris. The 2026 edition runs from May 25 to June 8. Clay-court tennis rewards endurance and baseline consistency, making it a distinct challenge from the other three Grand Slams.

Roland Garros is held at Stade Roland Garros in the 16th arrondissement of Paris, France, on outdoor clay courts.
