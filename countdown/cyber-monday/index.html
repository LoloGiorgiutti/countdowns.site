Cyber Monday is an online shopping holiday created by retailers in 2005 to encourage people to shop online. It falls on the Monday after US Thanksgiving (three days after Black Friday) and has grown into the biggest online shopping day of the year globally.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Cyber Monday 2026 is on Monday, November 30, 2026.

Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday?

Both offer major deals, but Cyber Monday focuses on online shopping and tech deals, while Black Friday traditionally focuses on in-store offers.
