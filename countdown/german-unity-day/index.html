German Unity Day (Tag der Deutschen Einheit) is Germany's national holiday, celebrated on October 3rd. It commemorates the reunification of East and West Germany on October 3, 1990, following the fall of the Berlin Wall in November 1989.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is German Unity Day?

German Unity Day is October 3rd every year.

What does October 3rd commemorate?

It marks the reunification of East Germany (GDR) and West Germany (FRG) in 1990.

When did the Berlin Wall fall?

The Berlin Wall fell on November 9, 1989, leading to German reunification less than a year later.
