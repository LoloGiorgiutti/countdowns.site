Father's Day in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada is celebrated on the third Sunday of June. It is a day to honour fathers and father figures with gifts, meals and quality time.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Father's Day 2026?

Father's Day 2026 (US/UK/Canada) falls on Sunday, June 21, 2026.

Is Father's Day in June or August?

In most countries including the US, UK and Canada, Father's Day is in June (third Sunday). Australia and New Zealand celebrate in September.
