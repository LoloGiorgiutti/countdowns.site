Jimmy Donaldson, known online as MrBeast, was born on May 7, in Greenville, North Carolina, USA. He is the most-subscribed individual creator on YouTube, with over 300 million subscribers across his main channel alone. MrBeast first gained viral attention in 2017 with his counting videos and quickly evolved into producing large-scale, high-budget challenge and philanthropy videos. He has given away millions of dollars in cash, cars, houses, and other prizes to his viewers. His philanthropic endeavors include planting 20 million trees through Team Trees, removing 30 million pounds of trash from oceans and beaches through Team Seas, and opening free food banks in the US. He has also launched MrBeast Burger and Feastables chocolate brand.
