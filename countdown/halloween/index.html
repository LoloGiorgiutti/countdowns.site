Halloween is celebrated annually on October 31, primarily in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Australia. Traditions include trick-or-treating, costume parties, pumpkin carving and haunted house visits.

When did Halloween originate?

Halloween has roots in the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain, later influenced by the Christian feast of All Hallows' Eve.
