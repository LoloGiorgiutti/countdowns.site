The MTV Video Music Awards (VMAs) are an annual awards ceremony presented by MTV to honor the best in music video production. First held in 1984, the VMAs are known for iconic performances and memorable moments in pop culture history. Categories include Video of the Year, Artist of the Year, Song of the Summer, and Best New Artist. The ceremony typically takes place in August or September. What is the MTV VMA Moonperson trophy?

The MTV VMA trophy, known as the Moonperson (formerly Moonman), depicts an astronaut planting an MTV flag on the moon. It is one of the most recognizable trophies in music.

Where are the MTV VMAs held?

The MTV VMAs have been held at various venues including the Barclays Center and Radio City Music Hall in New York.
