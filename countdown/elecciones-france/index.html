About French Presidential Election 2027

France holds presidential elections every five years. The next French presidential election is scheduled for spring 2027. The French president holds significant executive power — appointing the prime minister, leading foreign policy, and commanding the armed forces.

The election uses a two-round system: if no candidate wins an absolute majority in the first round, the top two candidates advance to a runoff two weeks later.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the French Presidential Election 2027?

The exact date is not yet confirmed. French presidential elections traditionally take place in April, with a runoff two weeks later if needed.

How does the French presidential election work?

It uses a two-round majority system. If no candidate wins 50%+ in the first round, the top two candidates face off in a second round.

Who can vote in the French election?

All French citizens aged 18 or older, including those living abroad, can vote.
