Angelina Jolie Voight was born on June 4, 1975, in Los Angeles, California, USA. A two-time Oscar winner, she first won Best Supporting Actress for Girl, Interrupted (1999) and received the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award in 2013. She became an international action superstar with the Lara Croft: Tomb Raider films and has delivered acclaimed performances in Girl, Interrupted, Changeling, Salt, Maleficent, and Eternals. Beyond acting, she has served as a UNHCR Goodwill Ambassador and later Special Envoy for over two decades, visiting refugees and internally displaced persons in more than 30 countries. She has six children, three biological and three adopted. Her public health advocacy, including her disclosure of a preventive double mastectomy in 2013 due to BRCA1 gene mutation, had a global impact known as the Angelina Effect.
