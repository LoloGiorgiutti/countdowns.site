Glastonbury Festival of Contemporary Performing Arts is held each June at Worthy Farm in Pilton, Somerset. Founded by Michael Eavis in 1970, it has grown into the world's largest greenfield music festival, hosting over 200,000 attendees across hundreds of stages. The 2026 edition runs June 24–28. Beyond music, Glastonbury features theatre, circus, cabaret, and a unique atmosphere unlike any other event.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Glastonbury 2026?

Glastonbury Festival 2026 runs from Wednesday June 24 to Sunday June 28, 2026 at Worthy Farm in Pilton, Somerset.

How do I get Glastonbury tickets?

Glastonbury tickets are sold through an annual registration and ballot system on the official website. Registration is free, but demand far exceeds supply — resale tickets often appear closer to the event.

Who has headlined Glastonbury?

Past headliners include Beyoncé, Paul McCartney, Coldplay, Radiohead, Adele, David Bowie, The Rolling Stones, Stormzy, and many more legends of popular music.
