Dwayne Douglas Johnson, known as The Rock, was born on May 2, 1972, in Hayward, California, USA. He first achieved fame as a professional wrestler in the WWE, becoming one of the most popular and iconic figures in professional wrestling history with his electrifying personality and catchphrase 'Can you smell what The Rock is cooking?' He transitioned to acting in the early 2000s and has become one of Hollywood's highest-paid actors, starring in blockbusters including the Fast & Furious franchise, Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle, Moana, Black Adam, and the Hobbs & Shaw spin-off. Known for his incredible physique and work ethic, Johnson is also a successful entrepreneur and motivational personality with over 400 million Instagram followers.
