The BAFTA Film Awards are presented annually by the British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA) to honor the best in cinema. First held in 1948, the BAFTAs are one of the world's most prestigious film awards and often serve as a strong predictor of Oscar outcomes. The ceremony traditionally takes place in February at the Royal Festival Hall in London. The 2027 date has not yet been announced.

BAFTA stands for British Academy of Film and Television Arts.
