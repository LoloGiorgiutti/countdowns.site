Scarlett Ingrid Johansson was born on November 22, 1984, in New York City, USA. One of the highest-paid actresses in the world, she began her career as a child actress and established herself as a major dramatic talent with Lost in Translation (2003) and Girl with a Pearl Earring (2003). She joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Natasha Romanoff/Black Widow in Iron Man 2 (2010) and went on to appear in over ten MCU films, becoming one of the franchise's most iconic characters. She has received two simultaneous Oscar nominations in the same year — for Marriage Story and Jojo Rabbit (both 2019). She is known for her distinctive voice, stage work, and her role as the voice of the AI in Her (2013). She is married to Saturday Night Live comedian Colin Jost.
