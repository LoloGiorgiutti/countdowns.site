Summer vacation marks the long school break that coincides with the warmest months of the year. In the Northern Hemisphere this typically begins in June, while in the Southern Hemisphere it starts in December. The countdown adapts to your selected country.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does summer vacation start?

Roughly mid-June in the Northern Hemisphere and mid-December in the Southern Hemisphere, varying by country.

How long is summer vacation?

Summer break typically lasts 6 to 12 weeks depending on the country.

Does the countdown adapt to my hemisphere?

Yes. Select your country and the countdown will show the approximate start of summer vacation for your region.
