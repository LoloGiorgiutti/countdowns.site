Dua Lipa was born on August 22, 1995, in London, United Kingdom, to Albanian parents from Kosovo. She began modelling at age 14 and signed with a talent agency at 15 before pursuing music full-time. Her self-titled debut album in 2017 produced the UK number-one single New Rules. Her second studio album Future Nostalgia (2020), released during the COVID-19 pandemic, became one of the best-reviewed albums of its year and produced global hits including Don't Start Now, Physical, Levitating, and Break My Heart. The album swept the 2021 Grammy Awards, winning Best Pop Vocal Album. Dua Lipa's disco-inflected sound and striking visual style have made her one of the defining pop stars of her generation. Her 31st birthday on August 22, 2026 will be celebrated by fans worldwide.
