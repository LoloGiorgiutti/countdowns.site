About Copa del Rey Final 2026

The Copa del Rey is Spain's premier national football cup competition, run by the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF). The tournament follows a knockout format, with clubs from all levels of Spanish football competing for the historic trophy.

The final is typically held in late April or May at a neutral venue, with hundreds of thousands watching across Spain and millions worldwide.

The two clubs that win their semi-finals. Traditionally Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, and Atlético de Madrid are strong contenders.

Where is the Copa del Rey Final held?

At a neutral Spanish stadium, chosen by the RFEF each year.
