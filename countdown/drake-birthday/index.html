Aubrey Drake Graham, known professionally as Drake, was born on October 24, 1986 in Toronto, Canada. He is one of the world's best-selling music artists, with over 170 million records sold. Drake is known for his versatility spanning hip-hop, R&B, and pop, with hits like 'God's Plan', 'Hotline Bling', 'One Dance', and 'Passionfruit'.

In 2026, Drake celebrates his milestone 40th birthday. He is one of the most-streamed artists in music history and a cultural icon of his generation.
