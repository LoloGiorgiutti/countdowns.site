Karol G (Carolina Giraldo Navarro) was born on February 14, 1991 in Medellín, Colombia. She is one of the most successful Latin urban artists of her generation, breaking records with her album Mañana Será Bonito. She was the first Latina to headline Coachella and has won multiple Latin Grammy Awards.
