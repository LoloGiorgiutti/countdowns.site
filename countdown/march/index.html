March is the third month of the year with 31 days. It marks the beginning of spring in the Northern Hemisphere (around March 20) and the start of autumn in the Southern Hemisphere. St. Patrick's Day falls on March 17.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does spring start in March?

The spring equinox in the Northern Hemisphere falls around March 20 or 21 each year.

How many days does March have?

March has 31 days.

What are the main March holidays?

St. Patrick's Day (Mar 17), International Women's Day (Mar 8), and the spring equinox.
