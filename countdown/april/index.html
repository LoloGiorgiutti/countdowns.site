April is the fourth month of the year with 30 days. It is known for April Fools' Day on April 1st. Easter often falls in April. In the Southern Hemisphere, April signals the beginning of autumn.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is April Fools' Day?

April 1st is a day traditionally marked by pranks and jokes. Its exact origin is uncertain.

How many days does April have?

April has 30 days.

Does Easter always fall in April?

Easter can fall between March 22 and April 25. It often lands in April.
