What is the Emmy Award?

The Emmy Award is given by the Television Academy to recognize excellence in American television. The Primetime Emmys cover network and streaming drama, comedy, limited series, and more.

Who has won the most Emmy Awards?

Among performers, Cloris Leachman holds the record with 8 Emmy wins. Among programs, Frasier (37 wins) and Veep (5 consecutive comedy wins) are among the most decorated.
