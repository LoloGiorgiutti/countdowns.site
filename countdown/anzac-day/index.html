ANZAC Day is observed every April 25 in Australia, New Zealand, and beyond. It commemorates the landing of the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps (ANZAC) at Gallipoli, Turkey, on April 25, 1915 — and honors all Australians and New Zealanders who served in wars, conflicts, and peacekeeping operations. Dawn services, marches, and ceremonies take place across both nations.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is ANZAC Day 2027?

ANZAC Day 2027 falls on Sunday, April 25, 2027. It is a public holiday in Australia and New Zealand every year on April 25.

What does ANZAC stand for?

ANZAC stands for Australian and New Zealand Army Corps. The term originated from the World War I landing at Gallipoli in 1915 and now refers to all service personnel from both nations.

How is ANZAC Day observed?

ANZAC Day is marked by dawn services at memorials, marches by veterans and their descendants, playing the Last Post, a minute of silence, and the traditional card game Two-Up.
