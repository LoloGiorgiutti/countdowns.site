Robert John Downey Jr was born on April 4, in Manhattan, New York City, USA. One of Hollywood's most celebrated actors, he began his career as a child actor in his father's films before rising to prominence in Chaplin (1992), earning his first Oscar nomination. After a turbulent period in the late 1990s due to substance abuse, he made one of Hollywood's greatest comebacks, appearing in Gothika, Kiss Kiss Bang Bang, and Zodiac. His casting as Tony Stark/Iron Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe transformed him into a global icon. He starred in over ten MCU films, becoming the cornerstone of the franchise. He returned to the MCU as Doctor Doom.
