The NBA Draft is the annual event where the 30 NBA teams take turns selecting players eligible to join the league. The draft consists of two rounds, with 60 players selected total. The order is determined by the NBA Draft Lottery for the teams that missed the playoffs.

The 2026 NBA Draft is expected to feature highly touted college players and international prospects competing to become the next stars of the league.
