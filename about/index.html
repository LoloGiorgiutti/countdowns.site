What is Countdowns.site?

Countdowns.site shows you the exact number of days, hours, minutes, and seconds until upcoming events worldwide. Every countdown updates in real time, every single second. The site automatically detects your country and shows the events most relevant to you.

It works across all devices and loads instantly. No account, no email, no payment — ever.

Key features

  • Live countdown — updates every second, not just every day
  • Your timezone, automatically — if Christmas starts at midnight where you are, that's when your countdown hits zero
  • Country-aware — Mother's Day, Children's Day, and national holidays show the correct date for your country
  • Shareable links — every countdown has a permanent URL you can bookmark or share
  • Embeddable — any countdown can be embedded on another website via iframe
  • Custom countdowns — create a personal countdown to any date and share it
  • 4 languages — full support for English, Spanish, Portuguese, and French
  • Dark and light mode

How are dates verified?

Every event date is sourced from official channels — UEFA for Champions League, FIFA for World Cup, Formula 1's official calendar for F1 races, and so on. For recurring events (Christmas, Easter, holidays), dates are calculated mathematically from confirmed formulas. Unconfirmed dates are always shown as "Date TBA" rather than guessed.
